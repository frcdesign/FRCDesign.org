We've compiled a list of custom community-made part files for different tools and guides to improve your team's organization or engineering process, separate from robot components that would be found in FRCDesignLib. 

<Aside type="tip">
The title of each custom part links to its respective file (on a file-sharing service). Report any missing links on the discord server.
</Aside>

## Recommended
These featurescripts are must haves to enhance your Onshape workflow. Many of these are developed by members of the FRC community and well maintained.

- Onshape document can be configured to create a different organizer for each belt size
- Automatic labeling driven by configurations
- Export a dxf for laser cutting or other 2D manufacturing processes
